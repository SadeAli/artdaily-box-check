$ man box-check

Freehand a 3D box, one straight stroke per edge — 6 edges minimum, a full box is 9–12 — then hit “check it”. “Undo” takes back the last stroke; “clear” starts the box over without spending the round.

Your strokes are sorted into the three edge directions and each is scored on convergence: a tight vanishing point scores high, parallel is fine (85), diverging is the classic mistake (caps at 30).

After checking, tap any critique row to spotlight just that set on the sheet. The dashed ghost is the true box the maths fitted to your best two vanishing points — the shape you were reaching for.

Studio tip: check yourself the way Drawabox does — extend every line after you draw it. Converging slightly beats parallel; diverging is never right.
